## Logistics

- **Purchase and scheduling** go through the Linux Foundation Training portal. You
  have **12 months** from purchase to take the exam, and **one retake** is included.
- **Delivery** is online and proctored. You must comply with the
  [Linux Foundation Global Certification & Confidentiality Agreement](https://docs.linuxfoundation.org/tc-docs/certification/lf-cert-agreement/),
  and should read the [Candidate Handbook](https://docs.linuxfoundation.org/tc-docs/certification/lf-handbook2)
  and [Important Instructions](https://docs.linuxfoundation.org/tc-docs/certification/important-instructions-mc)
  before exam day.
- **Duration** is **90 minutes** of multiple-choice questions.
- **Number of questions and passing score are not published.** Linux Foundation
  associate-level multiple-choice exams have typically been around 60 questions in
  90 minutes with a passing score around 75%. Treat those as planning assumptions,
  not facts: budget about **90 seconds per question** and aim for **85%+** on the
  mock exams.
- **Validity** is **two years**.

## Who it is for

The Linux Foundation positions MCPA for people moving into **AI engineering and
platform roles** and **AI governance and security roles**, and for teams building AI
assistants and enterprise AI solutions.

That split explains the domain weights. Engineering questions (architecture,
interactions) and governance questions (security, consent, auditability) together
make up most of the exam. Domain 4 alone is almost a quarter of it.
